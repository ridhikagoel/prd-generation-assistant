## Problem
We need to alert users of important events or changes in our platform.

## Goals & Non-Goals
| **Achieve** | **Not Try To Achieve** |
| --- | --- |
| Provide timely and relevant notifications to users. | Notify users about unrelated or non-critical information. |

## Target users
General users, support team.

## Requirements
* Send notifications at the right time (e.g., when a new message is sent)
* Make sure notifications are clear and concise
* Allow users to customize notification preferences

## Assumptions I made
* The notifications feature will be targeted at general users and the support team.
* Success of the notifications feature will be measured by user engagement with the notifications (e.g., clicks, responses).
* The scope of this feature does not include sending notifications about unrelated or non-critical information.

## Success metrics
* Average open rate of notifications
* Response time to notifications from users

## Open questions
How will we balance personalization and relevance in our notification system?

## Risks
* Overwhelming users with too many notifications, leading to fatigue
* Misclassifying notifications as spam or irrelevant content
* Technical issues causing delays or failures in sending notifications
